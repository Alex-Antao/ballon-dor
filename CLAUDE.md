# Ballon d'Or Lab — project context

Goal: three algorithms that pick the Ballon d'Or winner (Prediction, Fair, Mine), compared year by year with the real vote since 1956 on a public site, plus a daily-updated forecast of the next award (2027).
Language: talk to the user in European Portuguese; everything in the repository (code, comments, data notes, docs, file and identifier names, commit messages) is in English.

## Layout
- `data-src/*.json` + `eras.js` → `node gen-data.js` → `data.js` (1956–2026, 803 rows, fields described at its top) + `sources.json`. Format and rules: `data-src/SPEC.md`. Confidence A/B/C per row; `cont` (midfielders/defenders/GKs) is a subjective estimate.
- `models.js`: Prediction (`predict`, `PRED_FEATURES`, 19 criteria; called "Historical" until 30/10/2026; criterion 9 is `contTeam` = cont × max(continental, national-team stage) since 30/10/2026, see Step 3), Fair (individual only), Mine (shown as "Custom" on the site since 30/10/2026; individual + proportional team credit). Fair and Custom weight European goals/assists by the opponents (`uOpp`, option `oppW`, see Step 5); the Prediction keeps the competition weights. Runs in Node and in the browser.
- `dump.js` → `feats.json` → `fit_weights.py` (single LP fit with a hinge → `fit-result.json`) and `model_selection.py` (temporal model selection → `model-selection.json`; `--nested` adds the slow nested estimate, `--awards` the trophies/awards study (`awardStudy`, ~10 min), `--cont` the study of the subjective rating (`contStudy`, ~2 min), `--nations` the Nations League Finals study (`nlStudy`, ~1 min), `--extra` uses the reputation criteria, `--out FILE`). `extra_criteria.py` holds the 11 reputation/"star of the tournament" criteria (`EXTRA`) and the 8 trophy/award criteria (`AWARDS`). Python 3.14 with deps pinned in `requirements.txt` (numpy 2.5, scipy 1.18); on this machine run it as `py -3.14` (3.11 is still the default `python`).
- Nations League Finals per row (optional `nlGP`, `nlG`, `nlS`, 15 rows in 2019/2021/2023/2025): `node verify/nations-league.js [--write]` reads them from the Finals line-ups (verify/nt.js, `NL`). The models ignore them (`NL_W = 0` in models.js, see Step 4).
- Opponents of the European goals per row (optional `uOpp`, `uOppN`): `node verify/opponents.js [years] [--write]` reads them from the edition pages' match boxes; strengths from the UEFA club ranking (`verify/kassies.js`, Bert Kassies' database, cached in verify/work/kassies). The forecast computes them too (euSoFar).
- Trophies and awards per row (`cup`, `lgTop`, `gs`, `uTop`, `ntTop`, `ntBest`, `awConf`): `node verify/awards.js [blocks]` reads them from Wikipedia infoboxes and the Golden Shoe table (rules in the SPEC).
- Forecast: `forecast/update.js` (football-data.org via `forecast/football-data.js`, token in `FOOTBALL_DATA_TOKEN` env or `.env`; Wikipedia via verify/; assists of every candidate from the Football API on RapidAPI via `forecast/football-api.js`, key in `RAPIDAPI_KEY` env or `.env`, optional: one request per competition for its 100 best assisters (7 leagues + UCL/UEL/ECL = 10 a run; free plan 500 a month). The API's number wins over football-data.org's (mismatch = warning). A player missing from a list that ends at 1 assist gets 0 (aConf B, "0 or 1"); if the list ends higher his assists stay unknown; if football-data.org has a number for him, that number stays (a name that did not match). Offline: `FA_FIXTURES` (tests/fixtures/football-api); with `FD_FIXTURES` and no `FA_FIXTURES` the API is never called. UEL/ECL goals stay from the Wikipedia match boxes and a mismatch is a warning) → `forecast/2027.json` + `forecast/history.json`. Hand-kept candidates (non-scorers, cont, aliases) in `forecast/candidates.json`. Candidate pool: top 30 of the 7 leagues' scorer lists + the previous year's 30 Ballon d'Or nominees (Wikipedia; club from the infobox, exact name or `clubAliases`) + the hand-kept list; players outside the 7 leagues are dropped with a warning. Players on no scorer list get games/goals from this season's row of their Wikipedia career table (conf B; assists unknown). The models score the EXPECTED END OF SEASON (`expectLeague`: so far + remaining league games at a pace shrunk towards last season's rate, or a typical candidate's, with K = 10 games); the site shows the real numbers (gpNow/gNow/aNow) big and the estimate as a footnote. Decided 30/10/2026 after a review: scoring the raw partial numbers with full-season weights let any non-attacker with cont >= 8.8 beat every attacker in October. Hand-kept clubs are checked against the Wikipedia infobox every run (warning on mismatch). `WP_MAX_AGE_H` (12 h in update.js) re-fetches stale cached Wikipedia pages. Before Step 3 the Prediction forecast hinged on the subjective `cont` (Rodri 2027: cont 10 → 1st, 9.5 → 4th, 9 → 33rd); with `contTeam` the rating counts only once the team advances (no Euro/World Cup in 2027, so a European non-attacker needs a deep Champions League run: Rodri 9.5 is 44th in October, 2nd if Barça won the UCL). `FD_FIXTURES`, `NO_WIKI`, `AS_OF`, `FORECAST_OUT` for offline runs.
- Site: `site/` (index.html, styles.css, app.js) → `node build.js` → `dist/` (GitHub Pages at https://alex-antao.github.io/ballon-dor/; `sources.json` fetched only when a player's detail opens; the forecast goes into config.js). `node tools/serve.js` serves `dist/` on port 4173.
- Verification tools for the data: `verify/` (described in the SPEC).

## Workflow
`data-src/*.json` → `node gen-data.js` → `node dump.js > feats.json` → `python fit_weights.py` → `python model_selection.py [--nested]` → `npm test` → `npm run test:e2e` → `node build.js`.
If a change to the models or data is intended, refresh the golden rankings with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 npm test` and review the diff.

## Tests and CI
- `npm test` (node:test): `tests/unit/models.test.js` pins the reference results (Prediction 30/69 eligible and 30/69 "Everyone", Fair 20/69, Mine 23/69 with the single fit (24 before Step 5); temporal 9/16 in 2010–2026; 2026 → Kane) and every model's full ranking (`tests/golden/rankings.json`); `tests/unit/data.test.js` checks data-src against the SPEC and that `gen-data.js` reproduces `data.js`/`sources.json`.
- `tests/unit/forecast.test.js` runs the forecast offline on made-up responses (`tests/fixtures/football-data`, regenerate with `node tests/fixtures/make-football-data.js`).
- `npm run test:e2e` (Playwright 1.63, `playwright.config.js`): `tools/e2e-build.js` builds `dist/` with a forecast made from the fixtures, then runs `tests/e2e/site.spec.js` and `tests/e2e/overflow.spec.js` (no visible text may stick out of its card/tile/badge; the player detail is opened for every player of every year) on desktop Chromium, iPad Mini/iPhone 13 (WebKit) and Pixel 7. Install browsers once with `npx playwright install chromium webkit`.
- GitHub Actions: `ci.yml` (unit tests, data in sync, build, then e2e), `deploy.yml` (Pages after CI passes on main; needs a public repo and Pages source = GitHub Actions), `weights.yml` (reproduces `fit-result.json` and `model-selection.json` with `tools/check_results.py`, tolerance 2e-3, only when data/models/scripts change). `forecast.yml` (daily 06:30 UTC: update.js with the `FOOTBALL_DATA_TOKEN` secret, commit to main, `gh workflow run deploy.yml`). Dependabot monthly for npm, Actions and pip.

The Summary only counts awarded years. On the eligible list each year's Prediction uses that year's temporal weights (`temporal` in model-selection.json); 1956–1960 and the "Everyone" list use the single in-sample fit.

## Known gaps
- Reputation criteria and trophies/awards reach 15–16/16 when fitted to 2010–2026 but do not improve the honest forecast (see "Memorised fit" and "Trophies and awards" below).
- League strength is constant within a decade.
- What stays at confidence C is mostly before 1990 (players without a "Career statistics" table on English Wikipedia, tournaments without line-ups, Belgian titles, Argentina 1991–2014, West Germany before 1963, Messi 2026 in MLS). Assists stay estimated (aConf C).

## Step 1 (done) — data 1956–2025
User decisions: official top 10 per year + strong ineligibles (`elig: 0`, "Everyone" list); target of the "Everyone" list = France Football's retroactive winner (2016); window = calendar year up to 2021 (fallback `win: 'S'` → conf C), season from 2022; 2010–2021 redone as calendar years; assists estimated with `aConf`; league strength and cup weight per decade (eras.js); sources in sources.json; refits reported in-sample and LOO.
- Second automatic pass done (`verify/`): career table + season-page match boxes (calendar year counted match by match), league page (title, ≥ 1/3 of the games), European edition page (competition/stage by distance to the final), national-team line-ups. Manual corrections with reasons in `verify/overrides.json`.
- Confidence after the second pass: total A 52 / B 438 / C 313. 2010–2026: A 52 / B 116 / C 3; 1990–2009: B 166 / C 59; 1956–1989: B 156 / C 251. CY is at most B; seasons (2022+) can reach A. 178 rows on `win: "S"`.
- Refit with the second-pass data (fit-result.json):
  - train 2010–2025 (eligible): in-sample 14/15, LOO 9/15; only misses Modrić 2018 (→ Griezmann); 2026 → Mbappé.
  - train 1956–2025 (eligible): in-sample 30/69, LOO 27/69; 2010–2025 in-sample 8/15, LOO 8/15; 2026 → Kane.
  - train 1956–2025 ("Everyone"): in-sample 28/69, LOO 27/69; 2026 → Yamal.
  - Single fit: Prediction 30/69, Fair 20/69, Mine 24/69 (eligible). Fixed weights still do not explain 70 years of votes.
- Model selection (`model_selection.py`, TEMPORAL evaluation: to predict year Y, train only on years < Y). In 2010–2026 (16 years):
  - reference (19 criteria, train from 1956): 8/16.
  - window: from 1990/2000/2005 → 10/16; from 1956/1970/1980 → 8/16; rolling windows and recency weights do not help.
  - choosing criteria (backward elimination → 10 criteria) looked like 12/16, but making the choice only with each year's past gives 8/16 with mean rank 2.63: overfitting. The 19 criteria stay.
  - OFFICIAL PROCEDURE: 19 criteria; for each year pick the window/recency/reg that would best have predicted the previous 12 years → 9/16 without ever seeing the year (mean winner rank 1.88); 1961–2009 with the same rule 14/49. From 2013 it always picks "train from 2000". Model for 2027: train 2000–2026, reg 0.005, no recency. 2026 temporal forecast: Mbappé (Kane 2nd).
- "Memorised fit" (`memorisedFit`, fitting to the years being judged): with the 19 criteria the fit to 2010–2026 is 14/16 in-sample (misses Modrić 2018 and Messi 2023); adding ONE reputation criterion or "World Cup finalist who is not an attacker" → 16/16. Predicting unseen years none helps: 7–9/16 against 9/16 without them; all 11 together with window selection → 5/16. Conclusion: 16/16 is memorising; the Prediction keeps the 19 (`memorisedFit` in model-selection.json, table on the Method page).

## Step 3 (done 30/10/2026) — the subjective rating (cont)
- Problem: the 2027 weights were almost only isAtt and cont, so ±0.5 on one player's cont changed the pick. Study `model_selection.py --cont`, rule written BEFORE the first run: adopt only if 1961–2009 hits >= V0, 2010–2026 hits >= V0 and fewer fragile years (±0.5 cont on any one player changes the pick).
- V0 raw cont 14/49, 9/16 (rank 1.88), 18 fragile; V1 without cont 17/49 but 6/16; V2 without cont + non-attacker × team stage 16/49, 7/16; V3 cont × team stage 16/49, 9/16 (rank 1.81), 12 fragile; V4 raw cont + non-attacker × team stage 14/49, 7/16, 20 fragile. → V3 adopted (+2 hits in 1961–2009 after 4 tries is within luck; 1961–2009 mean rank 3.57 vs 3.31; the gain is robustness).
- After V3: official procedure 9/16 (hits 2012 and 2026 now, misses 2019 → Van Dijk and 2025 → Raphinha), 1961–2009 16/49; for 2010–2026 every window starts in 1995 or later; model for 2027: train 1980–2026, reg 0.005, no recency. Awards study on the new base: continental top scorer 19/49 but rank 2.56, trophy count 17/49, all awards 18/49, each 9/16 → still not adopted.

## Step 5 (30/10/2026) — opponents of the European goals (Fair and Custom)
- Asked by the user: weight output by the opponent, not by the competition (Benfica beating Milan in the Europa League). Normative choice for Fair and Custom only, so no study; the Prediction is fitted to the vote and keeps CUP_ERA.
- Per opponent: √(UEFA 5-year club coefficient used for that season's draws / that of the 32nd club), 0.3–2; per row the mean over the player's goals (else the club's matches); assists take the same value. The European bonus becomes euBonus × uOpp × (uG + aw·uA) (no league strength: the opponent is explicit) and Custom's uProd uses uOpp. `oppW` blends with the old weight (slider in the tuning column).
